About

I build from a founder’s perspective.

I’m Mahmoud Okal, a Shopify Developer & Media Buyer. I studied media buying with Ali Hesham and I’m also building my own e-commerce brand, A³ JEANS.

That means I don’t look at a store as code and design only. I think about the full system: traffic quality, customer journey, product page clarity, copywriting, speed, trust, and conversion potential.

My goal is simple: build a premium, light, fast, clear Shopify experience that helps the brand sell better.

Common weak points I fix

Unclear hero section Weak product page Generic copywriting Long customer journey Missing trust elements Slow website / high LCP
