My product-page method

Product page that doesn’t sell vs. product page that sells

A product page should not simply display a product. It should complete the sale started by the creative.

Case 01

NUMA Focus product page

The selling version makes the product value, offer, bundles, ingredients and next action easier to understand.

× Doesn’t sell
  • Weak value hierarchy
  • Limited benefit-led creatives
  • Offer is easy to miss
  • Little decision support
  • Weak cross-selling structure
  • Long, generic reading flow
VS
✓ Sells
  • Creatives show product value
  • Clear and attractive offer
  • Strong visual hierarchy
  • Bundle and subscription path
  • Social proof near the decision
  • Mobile-first purchase flow
Case 02

Dusk & Dawn product page

The selling version turns separate product information into one structured routine, offer and customer journey.

× Doesn’t sell
  • Information feels scattered
  • Offer is not prioritized
  • No guided routine journey
  • Weak product switching
  • Generic purchase area
  • Cross-sell appears too late
VS
✓ Sells
  • Clear product and routine structure
  • Offer is visible in the buying flow
  • Benefits and ingredients are grouped
  • Product switching supports discovery
  • Bundle upgrade increases AOV
  • Social proof strengthens trust
