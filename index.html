About

I build from a founder’s perspective.

I’m Mahmoud Okal, a Shopify Developer and E-commerce Strategist. I don’t approach a store like a normal developer who only cares about layout and code. I look at the full sales system behind the website.

Because I run my own e-commerce brand, A³ JEANS, and studied media buying with Ali Hesham, I understand what happens before and after the click: ad promise, landing page clarity, product page trust, mobile speed, checkout friction, and customer behavior.

My goal is simple: build premium, fast, and clear Shopify experiences that make the brand easier to trust, easier to understand, and easier to buy from.
